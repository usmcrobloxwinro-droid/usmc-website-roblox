About Us

One Corps. One Community. One Mission.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide an organized and immersive Roblox Marine Corps experience where members can participate in training, operations, leadership, and community events.

Our Values

Discipline, integrity, teamwork, leadership, professionalism, and commitment form the foundation of our community.

Divisions

Explore the commands within our organization.

01

TECOM

Training and Education Command responsible for developing Marines through structured training.

02

1st Marine Division

Ground combat command responsible for operational missions and field activities.

03

2nd Marine Division

A combat-focused division supporting operations, exercises, and community events.

04

3rd Marine Aircraft Wing

Aviation command focused on air operations, aviation training, and support.

05

Military Police

Responsible for maintaining order, enforcing community regulations, and supporting security.

06

MARSOC

Specialized command focused on advanced training and specialized operations.
